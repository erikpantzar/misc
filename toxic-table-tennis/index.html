Shakedown

A completely normal day

It starts the way every day in there starts. Someone confesses to driving front-wheel drive and is gently shunned for it. Someone else is on leg three of an endurance rally at two in the morning, having lost the same door twice in the same corner. There is admin: a link, a start list, an argument about whether tarmac is easier than gravel or just differently humiliating.

Then it's the small stuff. Which car. Which notes. Whether you're allowed to run a rally blind or whether that's basically recce with extra steps. steeze tags a mountain biker who has never opened the game, purely to grow the sport. oka22 mentions, with visible enjoyment, that there's one stage in there nobody has driven before and declines to say which. The only injuries anyone reports all afternoon are to bodywork and to a tyre that moved one centimetre and cost ten seconds.

Then all of a sudden lightning struck.

Not one bolt. Three, about forty minutes apart, into ground that had been dry for months.

  1. The R5 is a brick

    Frenzy regrets his car choice. Jorma calls the R5 a brick. Frenzy replies "respectfully, skill issue," then summons oka22 to adjudicate — 🎙️, "i'm waiting," 🕐, "guess i will never get an answer 😿." Jorma later admits the whole thing was bait.

  2. Are you still on the controller

    An innocent question to steeze, except it reopens an old file. Frenzy: "oh wait hes the guy who actually thinks controller isnt respectable, i remember."

  3. Taxi wasn't trying

    Taxi615 explains he lost fifteen seconds in cuts and was just having fun on stream. Frenzy calls that cope, then implies he's lying about not practising. Taxi finally goes off, and the afternoon stops being about cars.

What follows is quick. Voksy arrives with the older, larger grudge. Frenzy threatens to leak DMs proving Voksy is fake to people he still chats to daily, and adds that he won't, because Voksy doesn't want to lose the only online group he has. Jorma observes that drama arrives whenever Frenzy does and suggests DMs. Frenzy calls the server an echo chamber. The server responds by holding what oka22 immediately christens the Great Ban Party. M0r94n and Neiro turn up afterwards for the victory lap. Taxi delivers the closing statement: settle it in DMs, own your shit. oka22 asks for an AI summary of the whole thing. Hello.

Stewards' decision

The honest read

Frenzy is the clear points leader and it isn't close. But he wasn't shadowboxing. Jorma admits to baiting. oka22 spent the evening pouring petrol while smiling. And the last twenty minutes are a pile-on delivered to a man who can no longer reply, by two people who weren't there for any of it.

Everyone in that room would tell you it's just banter, and for about eighty percent of the afternoon they'd be right. The trouble is that the same joke lands differently depending on how much history is sitting underneath it, and there was a lot of history sitting underneath this one.

The tidiest line in the whole log is Taxi's: take it to DMs.
