How a model is made.

Read. Predict. Get taught manners.

Pretraining: read a huge pile of text, learn to predict the next word.

Post-training: fine-tuning on examples, people ranking answers (RLHF), Constitutional AI.

Appendix · Further reading

Weights and size.

Bigger is smarter.

And pricier.

Weights are the numbers a model learns in training.

More parameters, data and compute make better models, but bigger costs more to run.

Smaller models via distillation. Open-weight models you can run yourself.
