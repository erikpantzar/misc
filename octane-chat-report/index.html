Route

Where the conversation went

Numbered as stages because the drift is genuinely chronological. Nobody steered. It just went.

SS1

Hardware misery

Simagic wheelbase upgrades judged not worth it. Escalates into the RTX 5090 costing 7–8K in Taxi615's country — more than a car that stays in one piece for three months.

SS2

The €184,000 question

Someone posts a supercar listing. Proposals for what to do with it: crash it (Kromchy), admire it in the garage (Shotgun Willie), sign up for Historic Rally and drive it properly (Voksy the Wheel Destroyer).

SS3

Cars, real ones

Traction control renamed "the owner's button." Modern safety and comfort features declared sickening. Henry Ford's 1922 line about black paint gets quoted and immediately corrupted into "once you go black you never go back — Taxi, 2026."

SS4

The cat killer arc

Taxi615 posts GTA 5 cat carnage and is renamed by a second account that narrates the ensuing chase like a war correspondent until a military tank ends it. He insists he loves cats and is trolling. Nobody believes him.

SS5

Career planning, unexpectedly sincere

Toasty is pivoting into business analytics; Voksy Sychophant dumps SQL, Power BI and regex courses on him. Becomes a real conversation about pay: ~€900 average locally, €5k for the good jobs, versus Voksy's $1.5k per week.

SS6

Baltic diplomacy

BestHL calls a photo AI slop, gets branded "angry man from flat country," retaliates with lakes and blind crests. Resolved amicably: "We can only colectively hate france."

SS7

Climbing takes the channel

steeze and Toasty discover a shared hobby and the server becomes a bouldering forum for two solid hours. Tension boards, 45-degree walls, 5.13s, Nalle Hukkataival, and whether you should be afraid of falling.

SS8

Everyone is injured

Tennis elbow (two cases), a four-week-old torn lumbrical, cortisone injections to stop an arm locking at 90 degrees, and a snowbank incident sustained while trying to drift a front-wheel-drive car.
